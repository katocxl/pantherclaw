### G0 — M38 — Partners: MSSP partner access with customer-approved links, partner sessions and rollout proposals, and hash-only shared threat indicators — 2026-11-05

This brief is written before implementation (G0). It lists planned scope; delivery status is tracked by the M38 branches and, at the end of the milestone, in an exit status section. M38 is code-only like M30–M37: every stream codes on pushed branches first, and testing (the HR and T rows below) comes later.

**Purpose:** a managed security service provider (MSSP) can watch and help many customers from one org, and every org can share and use indicators of known-bad MCP servers, tool descriptions and injection text ([ADR-0065](../adr/0065-partners-and-shared-indicators.md); N19, N27). A customer approves each partner link with a security key, for at most 90 days, and revokes it at any time. Under a link the partner's people read what a support session reads and propose rollouts that only ever become drafts, every call recorded in the customer's audit log. A signed, hash-only indicator feed holds matching calls for a person, or only alerts; it never refuses.

**Founder decisions (2026-11-05, all recommended):**
- FQ38-1: an MSSP partner may read (what support access allows) and propose policy and pack rollouts. The customer's admin approves each one, and loosening still needs a second customer person.
- FQ38-2: a shared indicator match holds the call for a person and alerts. An org may switch to alert only. Never refuse.

**Coordinator decisions:**
- **D1100 Partner links (FD12, FQ38-1).** A partner is an ordinary org whose people are its members; partner and customer are on the same deployment. `pc.partner_links` (00420): partner and customer org, state REQUESTED, ACTIVE, DENIED, REVOKED or EXPIRED, scopes (READ always, PROPOSE optional), the requester, the decision with the deciding person and key, a window that starts at the approval and ends at most 90 days later (the request names 1 to 90 days), the revocation. RLS lets both orgs see a link and nobody else; a guard trigger moves states only forward and refuses a decision by the account that asked. `PartnerService`: RequestPartnerLink (partner, `partner.manage`), ListPartnerLinks and GetPartnerLink (`partner.read`), DecidePartnerLink (over the API always STEP_UP_REQUIRED: a customer person with `partner.approve` decides on `/partners/links/{id}/decide` with a WebAuthn step-up), RevokePartnerLink (customer, at any time, no step-up), RenewPartnerLink (a new REQUESTED link). A job expires links at their end and requests nobody decided within seven days. Permissions `partner.manage` (Org Admin), `partner.approve` (Org Admin, Security Admin; a person only), `partner.read` (both orgs, plus Auditor). Edition: the entitlement `PartnerAccess` (Enterprise), checked on the partner org; the customer needs none. Audit `partner.link_requested`, `_approved`, `_denied`, `_revoked`, `_expired`, recorded in both orgs.
- **D1100a (coordinator answers).** Opening a partner session needs a WebAuthn step-up within five minutes, as support Open, and its own permission `partner.operate` (a person only; Security Admin and Org Admin in the partner org; custom roles may grant it); `partner.read` stays for links and summaries. The cross-customer summary reads the partner's links in the partner org through the link policy and, per customer, one transaction in that customer's tenant that records `partner.summary_read` before reading the counts; `pc.cross_org_list` is not redefined. A partner session's principal is the partner org's user id, with audit actor `partner/<partner org>/<user id>`. ProposeRollout declares authenticated: a partner session needs its link's PROPOSE scope; from the partner org a person with `partner.manage` and PROPOSE on each link.
- **D1101 Partner sessions.** A partner person signed in to the partner org opens `/partners/customers/{org}/open`. Under an ACTIVE link this sets a partner session cookie (`pcn_`) scoped to the customer org, stored hashed in `pc.partner_sessions` (00420); it ends after 12 hours, 30 idle minutes or with the link. The caller is a principal of kind `partner`; `Human()` stays false in the customer org, so every human-only path, approvals included, refuses it. Each call re-checks the link (ACTIVE, inside the window), the partner org's edition and the partner person (enabled, still holding `partner.operate`), and writes `partner.call` in the customer org before it runs. rpcauth admits read-class procedures and `PartnerService.ProposeRollout` only. `ListPartnerCustomers` (partner side) returns, per ACTIVE link, the customer's id and name, the link's end, the waiting approvals and open alerts counts and the last decision time.
- **D1102 Rollout proposals (FQ38-1).** `pc.partner_proposals` (00421): link, partner and customer org, kind POLICY_VERSION or PACK, title, body (a policy document or a pack with its parameters, at most 256 KiB), an optional template id grouping one send to many customers, state PROPOSED, ACCEPTED, DECLINED, WITHDRAWN or EXPIRED (after 30 days), who proposed and decided, and the draft. ProposeRollout makes one row per customer over a link with PROPOSE. AcceptPartnerProposal (a customer person with `policy.author`) makes a DRAFT only: a policy version through the existing draft path (its `policy.version_created` record names `origin = partner:<partner org>`) or ApplyPack. DeclinePartnerProposal; WithdrawPartnerProposal (partner side). From there the existing publish and rollout flow applies. A PUBLISH or ROLLOUT change request for a version drafted from an accepted proposal always needs two distinct customer people with security keys; the fewer-than-two-publishers fallback does not apply, and with fewer than two eligible people it waits (`NEEDS_SECOND_PERSON`). Audit `partner.proposal_created`, `_accepted`, `_declined`, `_withdrawn`, `_expired`.
- **D1103 Threat-intel feed (FD13, FQ38-2).** Signed like the Hub: offline rootkey purposes `intel` and `intel-dev` (dev on loopback only) and an empty `internal/intel/roots.json`. The feed is a compact JWS, typ `pc-intel-feed+jwt`, over {spec `pc-intel/1`, a monotonic version, issued, expires at most 30 days later, indicators}. Each indicator has an id, a kind, a hex SHA-256 or shingles, a severity, techniques, a first-seen date and a summary of at most 280 characters. Kinds: `mcp_server` (SHA-256 of `lower(name)@version`, of `package_ref@version` or of an image digest), `tool_description` (SHA-256 of the normalized text: NFC, trimmed, whitespace collapsed, lowercased), `injection_text` (up to 64 SHA-256 of normalized 8-word shingles; a match needs at least min(3, n)). `pc.intel_settings` (consume OFF, HOLD or ALERT_ONLY, default OFF; share off by default), `pc.intel_state` and `pc.intel_indicators` (00422). Ingest offline (`pclaw-admin intel build` and `verify`, then `pantherclaw-server intel import --in`) or by a daily fetch of `intel.feed_url` (`PC_INTEL_FEED_URL`, https only) through the egress client (empty means off; `dev.intel_key_file` names a development key), verified against the roots, rollback refused, the set replaced atomically. Consuming needs Business; contributing works on every edition.
- **D1104 Matching (FQ38-2).** The upstream MCP `initialize` `serverInfo` is recorded on the connection's inventory (00423). `mcp_server` and `tool_description` indicators are checked at discovery and drift and on the decision path; HOLD adds a person's approval with reason `INTEL_MATCH_HOLD` through the content-signal hold, ALERT_ONLY only alerts, nothing ever refuses. `injection_text` shingles are matched in the content-signal scan. Pipeline `Version` 10. `pc.intel_matches` (00424), audit `intel.matched`, built-in detection `pc.intel.match` (alert only, HIGH).
- **D1105 Contribute and STIX.** ContributeIndicator (`intel.contribute`, Security Admin, a person, the org's share switch on) from a confirmed finding computes only the hash or shingles locally into `pc.intel_contributions` (00425). A finding is a quarantined server, package version or tool, or an inventory description the injection scan flagged (`FLAGGED_DESCRIPTION`). The operator's `pantherclaw-server intel export-contributions --out` writes JSON without org, account or any time finer than a day. ExportIntelSTIX returns a STIX 2.1 bundle of `indicator` objects with the pattern `[x-pantherclaw-indicator:sha256 = '<hex>']`. Permissions `intel.read`, `intel.manage`, `intel.contribute`.
- **D1106 Docs and assembly** as in this brief. **D1107 Streams** as in the table above; code-only, both in parallel.

**Threat map:** run `task docs:mitre` at assembly; no technique leaves the "No PantherClaw control yet" list unless the merged code maps one.

**Out of scope:** a separate MSSP edition or parent and child orgs, partner access across deployments, a partner acting as or approving for a customer person, partner changes that skip the customer's draft review, licensing for customers of partners (vision row 281), the hosted indicator feed and TAXII (Phase C), and refusing on a match.

**Security constraints.** Fail closed: a link needs a customer person's security key, a partner session re-checks its link, edition and person on every call and reads only, a proposal only drafts, and a partner-origin loosening always needs two customer people. Every partner call and summary read is in the customer's audit log. Only hashes enter or leave the indicator feed; consuming and sharing are off by default. Wording follows HR-681's list in code, CLI and docs.
