### G0 — M39 — Agent payments, sandbox only: AP2 mandates verified, agent request signatures, payment actions held for a person — 2026-11-05

This brief is written before implementation (G0). It lists planned scope; delivery status is tracked by the M39 branches and, at the end of the milestone, in an exit status section. M39 is code-only like M30–M36: every stream codes on pushed branches first, and testing (the HR and T rows below) comes later.

**Purpose:** agents start to pay for things with mandates their person signed ([ADR-0066](../adr/0066-agent-payments-verify-only.md); vision N23). PantherClaw governs the payer's agents: before a payment leaves, it verifies the AP2 mandate chain against the org's trust anchors, refuses a mandate that does not say exactly what the agent asks to pay, and holds every payment for a person unless the org set a merchant cap that allows it. For merchant customers it verifies agent request signatures (RFC 9421, Visa TAP and Mastercard Web Bot Auth shapes). Everything runs against sandbox anchors only; there is no live switch.

**Founder decisions (2026-11-05, all recommended):**
- FQ39-1: PantherClaw verifies mandates and never signs them for a person. It holds no card, network token or payment credential.
- FQ39-2: no agent payment goes through without a person by default. An org may allow amounts under a cap it sets per merchant.

**Coordinator decisions:**
- **D1120 Roles and limits (FD11, FQ39-1, FQ39-2).** PantherClaw governs the payer's agents and verifies agent signatures for merchant customers. It verifies and never signs; it stores no PAN, network token or payment credential, and payment instrument fields are kept only as SHA-256 digests. Sandbox only: every trust anchor must be marked sandbox and match a sandbox host allow-list in code (empty by default, plus loopback for the simulator); anything else is refused with `PAYMENTS_LIVE_REFUSED`, audited `security.payments_live_refused`, and trips the connection's circuit breaker like the Stripe live-mode check. No live switch exists. Mastercard agentic tokens are not verified (the network does that) and are never treated as proof (R-93).
- **D1121 Trust anchors.** `pc.payment_trust_anchors` (00430): kind `MANDATE_ISSUER`, `MERCHANT` or `AGENT_DIRECTORY`, a static JWKS or an https URL, sandbox (must be true), state `PENDING`, `ACTIVE` or `REVOKED`, with a guard trigger. `PaymentsService` Create, List, Get and Revoke; Activate over RPC always answers `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`, and activation happens on the core page `/integrations/payment-anchors/{id}/confirm` with a security key. Keys are fetched through the egress client with no redirects, a 64 KiB cap and a 1 hour cache. Algorithms: ES256 and EdDSA for JWTs, ed25519 and ecdsa-p256-sha256 for HTTP signatures; nothing else (no `none`, HS* or RSA-PKCS1 for mandates). Permissions `payments.manage` (Security Admin, people only) and `payments.read` (also Org Admin and Auditor). Business edition.
- **D1122 Mandate verification.** An in-house SD-JWT verifier (RFC 9901): disclosures with `_sd` and `_sd_alg` sha-256 only, a KB-JWT with typ `kb+jwt` checking aud, nonce, iat (at most 5 minutes) and sd_hash, and `cnf.jwk` holder binding. AP2 v0.2 checkout mandates (vct `mandate.checkout.1`, a merchant-signed `checkout_jwt` and `checkout_hash`) and payment mandates (vct `mandate.payment.1`: transaction_id is the hash of `checkout_jwt`, payee, payment_amount in minor units and currency, the payment instrument as a digest only); open mandates with `cnf` and the constraints `payment.amount_range`, `allowed_payees`, `budget` and `checkout.allowed_merchants`, where an unknown constraint fails, and an open → closed chain whose closed values must be unchanged. AP2 v0.1 CartMandate `merchant_authorization` (exp at most 15 minutes, cart_hash over the RFC 8785 JCS of the cart) and PaymentMandate `user_authorization` with a KB-JWT; an IntentMandate is informational and never proof. Issuers must be ACTIVE `MANDATE_ISSUER` anchors and a `checkout_jwt` signer an ACTIVE `MERCHANT` anchor. `ap2.Verify(ctx, raw, anchors, now)` returns `ap2.Verified` with the mandate digest `sha256:<hex>`; errors are `ap2.ErrInvalid` (`MANDATE_INVALID`, also for an expired mandate) and `ap2.ErrLiveRefused` (`PAYMENTS_LIVE_REFUSED`).
- **D1123 Agent request signatures.** An RFC 9421 verifier: `Signature-Input` and `Signature` covering at least `@authority` and `@path`, created and expires present, a window of at most 480 s, and a nonce cache (`pc.payment_sig_nonces`, 00431, unique per anchor and nonce, pruned after 10 minutes). Keys come from a Web Bot Auth directory or a Visa key service, only at an ACTIVE `AGENT_DIRECTORY` anchor's URL and never at a host the request names. Tags `agent-browser-auth`, `agent-payer-auth` and `agent-pay-auth`. TAP bodies (agenticConsumer, agenticPaymentContainer) must carry the header's nonce and keyid and be signed by the same key; paymentCredentialsHash is kept and never resolved. `VerifyAgentSignature` (payments.read or a service credential) answers valid, anchor, keyid, tag and agent claims; audited `payments.agent_signature_checked`.
- **D1124 Payment actions (FQ39-2).** An optional `payment: {amount_param, currency_param, merchant_param, mandate_param}` block marks an operation payment-class. **D1124a:** a new parameter type `mandate` (at most 32 KiB, hidden from policy) is allowed only as `payment.mandate_param`, and the dispatch template may send it; `amount_param` must be `money`, `currency_param` an enum or identifier, `merchant_param` an identifier, the four params distinct, the operation a write. Approvals show the verified facts plus "mandate (N bytes)". The gateway fact reader `paymentfacts` calls `ap2.Verify` on the mandate and reports `payments.mandate {mandate_valid, amount_minor, currency, merchant, payee, mandate_digest}`; on `ErrLiveRefused` it trips the connection's breaker. **D1124c:** a `pc.agentpay` connection's merchant endpoint must be on the same sandbox host allow-list as the anchors, else `PAYMENTS_LIVE_REFUSED` and the breaker trips; the connection carries `merchant_audience`, the audience the mandate's KB-JWT must name (`ap2.WithAudiences`). A pipeline step (pipeline Version 11) refuses a missing mandate (`MANDATE_REQUIRED`), an invalid or expired one (`MANDATE_INVALID`) and one whose amount, currency or merchant are not exactly the params (`MANDATE_MISMATCH`), and a mandate whose digest was already dispatched in the org (`MANDATE_REPLAYED`; **D1124b:** single use, recorded in `pc.payment_mandate_uses` in the transaction that records the payment, whether or not a person approved), then requires a person's approval unless an ACTIVE merchant cap allows the payment; the approval binds `mandate_digest`. Audit `payments.mandate_verified` and `payments.mandate_refused`; a successful dispatch records `payments.effect` evidence {mandate_digest, merchant, amount, currency, transaction_id, outcome}. Built-in detection `pc.payments.mandate_mismatch`, alert only.
- **D1124 Merchant caps (D1124a).** `pc.payment_merchant_caps` (00432): org, merchant (a normalized id), currency, `max_per_payment_minor`, `max_per_day_minor`, state `DRAFT`, `ACTIVE` or `RETIRED`, with a guard trigger (ACTIVE only from DRAFT with at least two distinct approvers and a change request; RETIRED is final). `PaymentCapService`: `ProposePaymentCap` (payments.manage, a person), `ListPaymentCaps` (payments.read), `RetirePaymentCap` (payments.manage). Creating or raising a cap is a loosening: it goes through a policy change request of kind `PAYMENT_CAP`, confirmed by two distinct people with security keys, and is voided with `TWO_PEOPLE_REQUIRED` when the org cannot supply two. A cap-allowed payment adds a budget debit owned by the cap (owner kind `payment_cap`, grouping `merchant`, period day, limit `max_per_day_minor`); only cap-allowed payments count. Over the cap is held for a person, never denied. The `merchant` grouping is also allowed on guardrail budget rules. Business edition.
- **D1124 `pc.agentpay`.** A reviewed sandbox package: `agentpay.checkout.submit` (params `merchant`, `amount`, `currency`, `checkout_jwt`, `payment_mandate`; a payment block; irreversible, one checkout per action, reconciled by a person on an unknown outcome) posts to the connection's merchant AP2 endpoint with an `Idempotency-Key`; `agentpay.mandate.inspect` reads the merchant's record of a transaction. The simulator `pantherclaw-sim agentpay` is a merchant plus a credentials provider with fixed development keys on loopback, with faults for a mismatched amount, an expired mandate and a declined checkout. `pantherclaw dev --agentpay-url` seeds the package and the connection `agentpay`.
- **D1125 Docs and numbers** as in this brief. **D1126 Streams** as in the table above; code-only.

**Threat map:** run `task docs:mitre` at assembly; payment mandate refusals are not mapped to a technique (`pc.payments.mandate_mismatch` carries `techniques_none`).

**Out of scope:** live payments and any live switch, signing a mandate for a person, holding or resolving a card, network token or payment credential, verifying Mastercard agentic tokens, IntentMandate as proof, agent payments over channels other than the gateway, refunds and disputes through AP2, and console screens (M40).

**Security constraints.** Fail closed: a missing, invalid, expired or mismatched mandate is refused before any decision; an anchor that is not a sandbox anchor refuses and trips the breaker; a cap allows a payment only when it is ACTIVE, approved by two people and the amount and the day's total fit, and otherwise the payment waits for a person. Policies never see the mandate; approvals and receipts carry its digest and verified facts only. Wording follows HR-681's list in code, CLI and docs.
